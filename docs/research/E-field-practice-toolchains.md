*Compiled **2026-10-11**. Scope: app ecosystems, GPU/OS features, and community best practice for local upscaling + SDR→HDR, with concrete parameters. Assumed target: Windows 11, one 12 GB NVIDIA GPU (RTX 4070 SUPER). **2026 releases are treated as the current frontier**; anything 2023 or earlier is explicitly flagged as older.*

## TL;DR

- **The offline still-image stack has converged on three tools**: **chaiNNer** (node graph, PyTorch/ONNX/NCNN/TensorRT), **Upscayl** (one-click Real-ESRGAN/Vulkan), and **ComfyUI** (diffusion-era SR — SUPIR and SeedVR2 are now native workflow templates). Everything else is a wrapper or a niche. [1][3][20]
- **The most important licence fact:** the *apps* are open (chaiNNer, Upscayl, Video2X, ComfyUI) but **models carry their own licences**, and community packs are frequently non-commercial. Upscayl's tracker has an open "Commercial use" question — unresolved field practice, not settled law. [5]
- **Real-time ≈ Anime4K-class shaders and driver VSR; offline ≈ diffusion/transformer SR. RTX VSR will not help a 4K source.** Anime4K's README states it is optimized only for **native 1080p anime (h.264/h.265/VC-1)**, is explicitly *not* an SRGAN replacement, and is not meant for downscaled 720p/480p/DVD. RTX VSR likewise engages mainly ≤1080p, does nothing at 4K, works best on 480p / low-bitrate 720p, often needs fullscreen, and on hybrid laptops you must force the browser onto the dGPU. [17][33] `[FULLTEXT]`
- **2026 frontier in one line:** Topaz shipped **two named 2026 model releases** ("The Precision Update", March 2026; "Next-Gen", April 2026 — billed as its largest single model release); **AMD announced FSR "Diamond" (FSR 5)** for RDNA 5 / next-gen consoles and **Intel published XeSS 3**; NTIRE ran its **eleventh** efficient-SR challenge; and the open-model scene moved toward **1× restoration (artifact-removal) models** and **extreme quantization** (e.g. a 1.58-bit Swin2SR ×2). [84][85][89][90][86][94][95]
- **RTX HDR still cannot HDR-ify a still image.** As of mid-2025 practitioners were still asking for exactly this; the working answers were (a) encode a video from the still and run **NVEncC `--vpp-ngx-truehdr`** (NVIDIA TrueHDR), or (b) play it in MPC-BE with RTX HDR enabled and screenshot to JXR. [63][24]
- **HDR wallpapers predate 2024** — Windows 11 "HDR background support" shipped in Insider build **22621.2191 / 22631.2191 (Aug 2023)** and requires a **.jxr (JPEG XR)** file. The feature is older than the 2024–26 window; only the surrounding tooling (gain maps, converters) is newer. [68]
- **The HDR-still complaint cluster is stable**: over-bright / blown highlights, banding, and washed-out SDR after enabling HDR. The last has two named third-party fixes (`HDR-Gamma-Fix`, and an AutoHotkey shim converting the SDR-in-HDR **piecewise sRGB** curve to **gamma 2.2**) — i.e. practitioners treat it as a Windows-side defect. [64][69]
- **Two-stage (2×→2×) vs single 4× is genuinely unresolved.** waifu2x-ncnn-vulkan carries an issue titled *"8x or 3 times 2x?"*, and Real-ESRGAN has a standing request for a **2× version of the anime 6B model**. Because several best-liked community anime models are 4×-only, "chained 2×" often secretly means upscale-4×-then-downscale. [14][12]
- **TTA is available everywhere and endorsed by nobody; denoise is baked into the model, not a slider.** `-x` exists in waifu2x-ncnn-vulkan, realcugan-ncnn-vulkan and realesrgan-ncnn-vulkan (an 8× flip/rotate average, no project claiming a quality win). Denoise instead appears as `waifu2x -n -1..3`, `realcugan -n -1..3`, or Real-CUGAN's separate `models-se`/`models-pro`/`models-nose` trees whose filenames encode strength (`up2x-no-denoise`, `up2x-conservative`, `up2x-denoise3x`). Picking the model *is* picking the denoise. [13][15][9] `[FULLTEXT]`
- **Debanding: `ffmpeg deband` is fine and cheap; `gradfun` is a trap for modern content — it is 8-bit only.** `gradfun`'s pixel-format list contains no 10/12/16-bit formats and it dithers internally with a fixed 8×8 ordered matrix. For 10-bit anime the community answer is **neo_f3kdb in VapourSynth**, not ffmpeg. [22][23][27][28]
- **Practitioners and papers disagree mainly about *what to measure*.** The 2025–26 critique literature is explicit: one position paper argues visual-processing evaluation should be "human-centered, not metric-centered", and separate work targets the "perception gap" in SR evaluation. Benchmarks remain dominated by bicubic-degraded natural-image sets; flat cel-shaded art, line art and high-key illustration are barely represented. [71][72]
- **The cheapest high-value change is not a bigger model — it is deleting halos.** Anime4K ships `Clamp_Highlights` specifically "to prevent overshoot and reduce ringing" and recommends it by default, and its `Deblur` is defined as sharpening "without overshoot or ringing". Over-sharpening line art is the most common self-inflicted wound. [18] `[FULLTEXT]`

## Real-time vs offline

### What genuinely runs at playback time

- **Anime4K GLSL in mpv** is the reference implementation. Frame budgets are explicit: **24 fps → 41 ms, 30 fps → 33 ms, 60 fps → 16 ms**; verify with the mpv profiler (Shift+I then 2). Each CNN size step **doubles** shader time (M→L→VL→UL); a shader placed *after* an upscale costs **4×** what it costs before. [18] `[FULLTEXT]`
- **Chain rules from the project itself:** include `Clamp_Highlights` at the front by default; never load the same shader file twice (buggy + slow); shaders run in the order given in `input.conf`; use `AutoDownscalePre_x2/x4` between upscale stages so the second stage works at screen size — "improves performance without noticeably impacting quality". [18] `[FULLTEXT]`
- **Mode triage (condensed, verbatim intent):** **A** = most 1080p anime, some 720p, most old SD; high blur, resampling artifacts, compression smearing → *can amplify ringing and banding*. **B** = some 1080p, most 720p, 1080p→720p downscales; low blur, some resampling artifacts, ringing → *reduces ringing and aliasing*. **C** = 1080p→480p downscales, **no degradation, wallpapers, Pixiv art**; *highest PSNR, lowest perceptual quality*. Secondary modes (`A+A`, `B+B`, `C+A`) are for ratios **≥2×** only — using A+A on a 1080p source on a 1080p screen "will most likely oversharpen and degrade the image". [18] `[FULLTEXT]`
- **NVIDIA RTX VSR** runs in the driver/NGX path and needs an **RTX** card (Tensor Cores). Configured in NVIDIA Control Panel → *Video* → *Adjust video image settings*, or the NVIDIA App (*System → Video*), where the **status indicator** toggle is the practical way to confirm it engaged (an "RTX VSR" chip over the video). Quality is a 1–4 ladder (higher = heavier). Behaviour: engages mostly ≤1080p, **no effect at 4K**, best on 480p and low-bitrate 720p, works on local files dragged into a browser; hybrid laptops must force the browser to the dGPU via *Settings → System → Display → Graphics → [browser] → High performance*. [33][34]
- **The RTX Video "SDK" is the Maxine/VFX SDK's `VideoSuperResolution` filter** — the same NGX effect exposed to applications — plus **TrueHDR** for SDR→HDR, which is what `--vpp-ngx-truehdr` and RTXVideoProcessor invoke. [35][36][24]
- **RTX HDR** is the HDR sibling (per-game and RTX-Video-HDR for browsers/video). It **does not accept a still image**: the Chiphell thread asking for exactly that ended with the NVEncC-TrueHDR workaround and the "video → MPC-BE with RTX HDR → screenshot as JXR" workaround, and the original poster had already noted RTX HDR "only supports video→HDR". [63] `[ANECDOTAL but technically specific]`
- **Windows Auto HDR** is the zero-effort baseline (DX10/11/12-class titles; originally announced for DX11/DX12 on Windows 10 — *older, 2021*). Microsoft ships a **known-issue entry stating Auto HDR may cause games to stop responding or display incorrect colours** — "free HDR" has a documented correctness cost, which is the standard argument for replacing it with **Special K** or **RenoDX**. [41][38]
- **Special K HDR** injects a DLL into the game (local proxy-DLL or global CBT hook; Windows 8.1+, or WINE with `UsingWINE=true` in the per-game INI). Its wiki documents an **"HDR Retrofit"** path explicitly covering **DirectX 9** games — older titles Auto HDR/RTX HDR cannot reach. [45][46] `[FULLTEXT for mechanism]`
- **ReShade-side retrofit** is the third lane: **Lilium HDR Boost** ships as a ReShade shader on Nexus Mods; **PumboAutoHDR** is an "advanced ReShade AutoHDR"; **RHI** is a ReShade HDR installer; `hdrmods.com` publishes an **HDR Shader Order** page because stacking order changes the result. Community "all HDR tools" tutorials now cover the whole set side by side (RenoDX / Special K / RTX HDR / Auto HDR). [50][48][49][51]
- **Windows AI APIs** now expose an AI-imaging surface with a dedicated **Image Super Resolution** API, and DirectML ships a **DirectMLSuperResolution** sample. These are *application* APIs (Copilot+/NPU-class story), not a user-facing wallpaper/video toggle — relevant if you are writing an app, not enhancing a file. [39][40]
- **Other real-time routes:** Anime4K WebExtension does anime upscaling inside Firefox [53]; Magpie does it for arbitrary Windows windows (games, video players, emulators) [52]; **Jellyfin's upscaler plugin** moves it server-side for a media library [92]; **Stremio-Kai** ships an `anime4k-svp` release combining Anime4K shaders with SVP frame interpolation inside a streaming client [93]; and mpv builds now pre-bundle Anime4K + ArtCNN instead of expecting users to assemble shader folders [96].
- **Roadmap history matters for expectations.** VSR arrived with driver 531.18 (early 2023) for RTX 30/40; a later update improved detail preservation, **extended VSR to RTX 20-series** and added an **OBS broadcast** integration — so older how-to pages give the wrong GPU list `[older — 2023]` [88]. At the 2026 frontier, AMD announced **FSR "Diamond" (FSR 5)** for RDNA 5 / next-gen consoles after the Dec-2025 **"FSR Redstone"** driver work [89][44], and Intel published **XeSS 3** for developers with matching Arc driver notes [90][43]. Framing caveat: FSR/XeSS are *renderer* upscalers (games), not video-playback super-resolution — the RTX-VSR-equivalent for AMD/Intel remains DIY shaders or NCNN tools.

### What still has to be offline

- Anything diffusion/transformer (SUPIR, SeedVR2, StableSR-class) — seconds-to-minutes per frame, not milliseconds. [20][21]
- Multi-pass model chains, TTA, and tile sizes chosen for quality rather than latency. [13][15]
- **Restoration of genuinely degraded sources.** Anime4K's README is blunt: even if it might work, it is *not* optimized for downscaled 720p/480p/SD, and pre-digital anime carries "bad deinterlacing, camera blur during production, severe ringing, film grain, older MPEG compression artifacts" that real-time shaders do not fix. [17] `[FULLTEXT]`
- HDR-ification of stills (see the RTX HDR note above) and any per-shot HDR grading. [63]

### Honest quality ceiling

Real-time shaders buy **line reconstruction, deblurring, denoising and ringing/aliasing reduction** at 1080p→4K. They do **not** invent plausible texture and cannot recover detail that was never encoded. Offline GANs buy texture but risk structural drift; offline diffusion buys the most detail and risks *inventing content*. Practitioner split: **real-time for watching, GAN for archiving, diffusion only when reinterpretation is acceptable** — and never diffusion on flat cel-shaded line art you intend to keep faithful. [17][18][20][71]

## Community workflow recipes (concrete parameter advice)

### R1 · Anime / illustration still (faithful, archival)
1. **Identify the degradation first, then pick the model.** Line art + compression noise → `RealESRGAN_x4plus_anime_6B` / `realesrgan-x4plus-anime` or Real-CUGAN. Soft-shaded illustration → Real-CUGAN `models-se`, or the highest-rated community RCAN/RPLKSR anime models on OpenModelDB. Photographic → `RealESRGAN_x4plus` / `4x-UltraSharp` / `Remacri`. Upscayl's bundled HFA2k / Remacri / UltraSharp / Ultramix set is a reasonable frozen shortlist. [3][9][15][61]
2. **Denoise is a model/level choice, not a post-step.** `waifu2x-ncnn-vulkan -n 1..3` (default 0; `-1` = off); `realcugan-ncnn-vulkan -n -1..3` (default `-1`) or the equivalent `models-*/up2x-denoise1x|2x|3x` file; Real-ESRGAN's `realesr-general-x4v3` exposes a **`-dn` denoise-strength** knob. Clean CG renders and rescans want `no-denoise`/`conservative`; webtoon/JPEG soup wants `denoise2x/3x`. [13][15][9] `[FULLTEXT]`
3. **Then upscale.** Do not sharpen before upscaling — that feeds halos into the network.
4. **`--outscale` is not native arbitrary scale.** Real-ESRGAN's Python CLI resizes the 4× output with **LANCZOS4**; the ncnn build's `-s` accepts only 2/3/4. So "3.5×" means "4× then resample", which re-introduces softness. [9] `[FULLTEXT]`
5. **If you need 4× and your chosen model is 4×-only, do not chain 2×+2× "because it's better" — chain only when the 2× model is the one you actually want.** The community is not settled (see Debates). [14][12]
6. **TTA (`-x`) exists in all three ncnn tools and is 8× compute.** Reserve it for a final archival pass on a hero image, not a batch. `[FULLTEXT for existence; UNVERIFIED for benefit]` [13][15][9]
7. **Tiles: start at `-t 0` (auto).** On OOM or all-black output with a non-NVIDIA GPU, set a manual tile (chaiNNer's notes attribute all-black NCNN output to the driver crashing after OOM). Smaller tiles = less VRAM, more seams; the Real-ESRGAN ncnn README warns tiling can cause **block inconsistency** and divergence from the PyTorch result. So with enough VRAM prefer large tiles/`0`, and prefer PyTorch over NCNN when exact output matters. [13][15][9][1] `[FULLTEXT]`
8. **Sharpen after upscale, gently, small radius.** The visual failure on line art is *halos along black outlines*, not lack of micro-contrast. Anime4K encodes this: `Clamp_Highlights` "computes and saves image statistics … then clamps the image highlights at the end after all the shaders **to prevent overshoot and reduce ringing**", and `Deblur` "sharpens details without overshoot or ringing"; `Darken`/`Thin` are explicitly taste controls. `[FULLTEXT for mechanism; specific radius/amount figures are UNVERIFIED — see Debates]` [18]
9. **Fail loudly, not quietly.** Darken/Thin "might darken/thin other stuff" because "what constitutes a line is ambiguous" — always A/B against the source at 100 % zoom before shipping. [18] `[FULLTEXT]`

### R2 · Photo still
Same order, different picks: `4x-UltraSharp` / `Remacri` / HFA2k (Upscayl) or Real-ESRGAN `x4plus`; skip anime-specific denoise; consider face restoration (GFPGAN lineage is wired into Real-ESRGAN's own pipeline via `--face_enhance`). Upscayl's FAQ is worth internalising: these tools **cannot de-blur or fix defocus** — if the source is soft, you are enlarging softness. [3][9] `[FULLTEXT]`

### R3 · Anime video (offline, free)
1. **Prefer a video-native tool over frame extraction.** **Video2X 6.4** does Anime4K v4 *and* arbitrary mpv GLSL shaders *and* Real-ESRGAN/Real-CUGAN/RIFE via ncnn+Vulkan, with no extra disk usage. [6][7]
2. **Classic frame-based path (still widely used):** `ffmpeg -i in.mp4 -qscale:v 1 -qmin 1 -qmax 1 -vsync 0 tmp_frames/frame%08d.png` → upscale → `ffmpeg -i out_frames/frame%08d.jpg -i in.mp4 -map 0:v:0 -map 1:a:0 -c:a copy -c:v libx264 -r <fps> -pix_fmt yuv420p out.mp4`. Note the official example writes **JPEG frames** (`-f jpg`) — use PNG/16-bit for archival. Real-ESRGAN's video script supports `--num_process_per_gpu` because **the bottleneck is I/O, not the GPU**. [11] `[FULLTEXT]`
3. **Pick the anime video model, not the image model:** `realesr-animevideov3` is an XS-size model that also supports ×1/×2/×3 — i.e. **it can run at ×1 as a pure denoiser/restorer**, an under-used trick. [11] `[FULLTEXT]`
4. **Consider not re-encoding at all.** Anime4K's README states the design intent outright: re-encoding anime to 4K "should be avoided as it is non-reversible, potentially damages original content by introducing artifacts, takes up to O(n²) more disk space" without meaningfully reducing entropy. If you only need to *watch* it, use the shaders. [17] `[FULLTEXT]`

### R4 · Debanding and dithering
- **ffmpeg `deband`** — exact defaults from source: `1thr..4thr = 0.02` (range 0.00003–0.5), `range = 16`, `direction = 2π`, `blur = 1`, `coupling = 0`. There is **no `planes` option** (a common misconception); per-plane control is via `1thr/2thr/3thr/4thr`, and `coupling=1` forces 4:4:4-class formats and decides jointly across planes. Thresholds are scaled by bit depth internally, so `0.02` is depth-relative and works on 10-bit. `blur=1` uses the 4-neighbour average with one `|src-avg| < thr` test; `blur=0` requires **all four** neighbours within threshold (much more conservative). [22] `[FULLTEXT]`
- **ffmpeg `gradfun`** — defaults `strength = 1.2` (0.51–64), `radius = 16` (4–32, clamped even). **Only 8-bit-ish formats** (YUV410P/420P/422P/444P/440P/411P, GRAY8, GBRP) — no 10/12/16-bit — and it **dithers back to 8-bit internally with a fixed 8×8 ordered matrix**. It also no-ops when `min(w,h) ≤ 2*radius`. Fine for an 8-bit SDR delivery step; the wrong tool for 10-bit anime masters. [23] `[FULLTEXT]`
- **Dedicated debanders are the anime-community norm.** `flash3kyuu_deband` (f3kdb) has versioned documentation, and its maintained fork is **neo_f3kdb**; Doom9 has a standing thread on "Debanding anime with VapourSynth and neo_f3kdb". NVEncC exposes both `--vpp-deband` and `--vpp-libplacebo-deband` for parity inside an encode. [26][27][28][24]
- **f3kdb parameter values** circulate as `range≈12–24`, `Y≈48–64`, `Cb/Cr≈24–32`, `sample_mode=2`, `dither_algo=3`, plus small grain for gradients. **`[UNVERIFIED]` — the f3kdb docs were unreachable this session**; treat these as the community's conventional starting point and confirm against f3kdb 2.0's own docs. [26] `[SNIPPET]`
- **Dithering:** practitioners mostly do not hand-dither. It arrives implicitly — `gradfun` dithers internally, and depth/format conversion in swscale is ordered-dithered. Where people *do* care: **gradient-heavy anime skies**, **8-bit output from a 10-bit pipeline**, and **HDR gradients**. The rule is "**keep 10-bit end-to-end and you rarely need to think about dithering**"; if you must land on 8-bit, dither last, after debanding. `[INFERRED from the gradfun implementation + forum practice]` [23]
- **Order for video:** denoise → deband → upscale → sharpen → (HDR/texture/encode). NVEncC enforces a **fixed VPP order regardless of command-line order**, a good sanity model to copy: deinterlace → colorspace → libplacebo tonemapping → … → degrain/denoise → deband → resize/unsharp/CAS → `ngx-truehdr` → onnx/anime4k. [24] `[FULLTEXT]`

### R5 · HDR stills / HDR wallpapers on Windows 11
1. **Format reality check:** Windows 11 HDR wallpapers require **JPEG XR (.jxr)**; the feature landed in Insider **build 22621.2191 / 22631.2191 (Aug 2023)** — *older than the 2024–26 window*. [68]
2. **Authoring path:** grade in a real HDR tool (Lightroom/ACR/Photoshop 32-bit, or Resolve), export HDR (EXR / 16-bit), then convert to JXR. **`JxrEncApp` from `libjxr-tools`** is the canonical CLI converter; hosted converters such as **ExrToJxr** exist, and **`wallpaper-auto-hdr`** is a purpose-built tool that converts an SDR desktop wallpaper to HDR directly. The reverse direction — for inspection — is **`ledoge/jxr_to_png`** and **`ledoge/jxr_to_avif`**. [65][66][67][91]
3. **Phones are not a shortcut.** "How do I convert an Android Ultra HDR JPEG to JXR for Windows 11 HDR wallpaper?" is a recurring question; the HDR data lives in a **gain map**, so a naive decode silently drops it. `[SNIPPET-level; the question recurs across sources]`
4. **Validate with a real HDR viewer, not the desktop.** **HDR + WCG Image Viewer** (ledoge) is the community's standard inspection tool; `jxr_to_png`/`jxr_to_avif` let you diff levels and check clipping. Always confirm the wallpaper is actually treated as HDR and not tone-mapped. `[SNIPPET]` [66]
5. **The complaint list (recurring and specific):**
   - **Over-bright / blown highlights** — NGA threads titled essentially "I screenshotted a bunch and they're all overexposed". HDR derived from SDR is very easy to over-lift. [64]
   - **Washed-out SDR after enabling HDR** — a *Windows-wide* complaint, not a wallpaper one: Intel publishes a KB literally titled "After Enabling HDR in Windows, the Colors Are Washed Out", and the community shipped **two named fixes** for the SDR-in-HDR curve (an AutoHotkey piecewise-sRGB→gamma 2.2 shim, and the **HDR-Gamma-Fix** tray utility). That the fix exists as third-party software is strong evidence practitioners consider the default mapping wrong. [42][69]
   - **Banding** — the classic SDR→HDR failure on smooth gradients; mitigations are 10-bit minimum, deband before the lift, dither at output. (Cross-references sibling report C, failure modes F9/F10.) [22][23]
   - **Wallpaper-engine interaction** — HDR bloom artifacts on animated wallpapers are a known user report. [70] `[ANECDOTAL]`
6. **RTX HDR cannot do this for you** (video only); the two documented workarounds are NVEncC `--vpp-ngx-truehdr`, or the MPC-BE-screenshot-to-JXR trick. For **stills**, the practical answer remains real HDR authoring + JXR conversion, or an ITM model (see report C). [63][24]
7. **Keep the SDR original.** Whatever you ship, keep the SDR base so the fallback is not worse than the source — the most-repeated lesson from the ITM literature, and it costs nothing.

## Debates and biases

**1 · Benchmark winners vs things that look good.** Two 2025–26 papers attack the evaluation stack head-on: a position paper arguing that *evaluation of visual processing should be human-centered, not metric-centered*, and work on the **"perception gap" in image super-resolution evaluation**. Practitioner consequence: GAN/diffusion models that "win" on perceptual metrics are often the ones that invent texture, while the models people actually archive with are more conservative. `[SNIPPET]` [71][72]

**2 · Dataset bias is structural, not incidental.** The classic ladder — Set5/Set14/Urban100/**Manga109**, plus DIV2K-derived sets — is built on **bicubic downsampling of natural images**. Real degradation (JPEG, screen resampling, double compression, scanner blur) is a different distribution, which is exactly why Real-ESRGAN built a synthetic *degradation pipeline* and why the field moved to RealSR/DRealSR/real-world tracks. **Manga109 is the only manga-adjacent set in the classic ladder**, and it is black-and-white line work — not colour cel-shaded animation, not flat high-key illustration, not UI/text-heavy graphics. "SOTA on Manga109" does not transfer to the content a practitioner actually has. `[SNIPPET/INFERRED]` [73][74][62]

**3 · NTIRE/AIM moved toward the real problem; the everyday leaderboards did not.** NTIRE 2025 formalised efficiency and RAW-domain realism; by **CVPRW 2026** the workshop had run an **eleventh** efficient-SR challenge and added 3D-content and light-field SR tracks [73][74][86][87]. Two structural signals in 2026: the frontier is **efficiency** (not peak PSNR) and it is **task-branching** (3D, light field, RAW) rather than better natural-image ×4. Meanwhile the open-model scene drifted in a different direction entirely — toward **1× restoration / de-artifact models** and **extreme quantization** [94][95] — because that is what practitioners actually need. Practitioner reading: *interesting evaluation moved to challenges, while "which model looks best on my image" is still answered by eyeballing community comparison pages.*

**4 · 2× chained vs single 4×.** Genuinely unresolved. Evidence it matters: (a) waifu2x-ncnn-vulkan issue *"8x or 3 times 2x?"*; (b) Real-ESRGAN's standing request for a **2× anime_6B** model, i.e. users want to chain the model they like at 2× instead of being forced into a 4× pass; (c) several best-liked community anime models are 4×-only, so "chained 2×" often means upscale-4×-then-downscale, wasting compute and softening output. **Rule of thumb to test locally, not a law:** chain 2× when a native 2× model exists for your content *and* you need exactly 4×; otherwise use the 4× model directly and only downscale for non-power-of-2 targets. `[FULLTEXT for the artefacts; INFERRED for the rule]` [14][12]

**5 · TTA: documented, endorsed by nobody.** `-x` appears in waifu2x-ncnn-vulkan, realcugan-ncnn-vulkan and realesrgan-ncnn-vulkan — three independent tools exposing the same 8×-cost option, with **no project claiming a quality win**. Community consensus treats it as a "try it on one image" curiosity. Any TTA-superiority claim is `[UNVERIFIED]`. [13][15][9] `[FULLTEXT]`

**6 · Denoise-then-upscale vs upscale-then-denoise.** The anime tooling answers this by design: denoise is *inside* the upscaler (`waifu2x -n`, Real-CUGAN `models-*/denoise*`, Real-ESRGAN `-dn`), i.e. **jointly, before detail synthesis**. The failure mode of upscale-then-denoise is that the upscaler has already amplified blocking/ringing into detail-like structure the denoiser then reads as signal. If you must separate the stages, **denoise first, with a model trained for it** (SCUNet-class for heavy noise — see chaiNNer's own issue traffic), then upscale. `[FULLTEXT for tool design; INFERRED for the ordering rule]` [13][15][9][1]

**7 · Tiling: seams are a documented artefact.** Real-ESRGAN's README states the ncnn tiled path "may introduce block inconsistency (and also generate slightly different results from the PyTorch implementation)". The trade is VRAM vs consistency: **use the largest tile that fits**; if seams appear, raise the tile or switch to PyTorch/TensorRT. `[FULLTEXT]` [9][1]

**8 · Sharpening values — no canonical number exists.** Searches for "unsharp radius/amount best practice" surface Photoshop's general guidance ("radius varies according to subject matter, output size and output method") rather than any upscaling-specific consensus. What *is* consistent across primary sources is **mechanism-level** advice (small radius, avoid overshoot, clamp highlights). Treat any published "amount 0.5 / radius 1.5" figure as folklore unless it ships with comparison images. `[UNVERIFIED]` [18]

**9 · Diffusion upscalers and content invention.** SUPIR/SeedVR2-class models are the most capable and least faithful; the complaint is invented faces/text and stylistically illegal texture on cel art. Practice splits by **use case**: creative upscaling (cloud/diffusion — Magnific/Krea/SUPIR) vs archival faithfulness (GAN/NCNN — Real-ESRGAN/Real-CUGAN/chaiNNer). `[ANECDOTAL, widely repeated]` [60][75][20][21]

**10 · Commercial-tool trust is at a low point.** Topaz's move to subscription produced a long "Topaz Going Subscription" thread, and "can I get this locally and free?" is now the default framing — precisely why chaiNNer/Upscayl/ComfyUI/Video2X dominate this report. [59][75][77]

**11 · Beware AI-generated "comparison" content.** Several search results were machine-generated articles with precise-looking PSNR/SSIM numbers and no methodology — e.g. a Dec-2025 "Waifu2x-Extension-GUI 横评" claiming specific dB figures and a "0–10 denoise slider", which does not match the tool's actual per-engine `-n -1..3` ladder. The Chinese dev-blog ecosystem (gitcode blogs, some cloud-vendor community posts) is heavily AIGC. **Do not cite their numbers.** `[AI-GEN]` [77][81]
